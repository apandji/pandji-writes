# What we did
- Define problem, persona, and user journey again after a few months away. Clear feature-set with a roadmap based on MOSCOW prioritization. 
- Identified opportunities to interject: moment of moving to a new place, and ways to encourage journaling.
- Defined language of logs, journals, enrichment, etc. 
- Design exploration around felt / dots concept
- Went back to some of the things we heard during concept/provocation testing and used that to build out a user journey that we can defend.

# What we learned
- There’s a lot of richness in our interviews from last year. We should be in them more often.
- Thinking through the details is important particularly in encouraging people to log and journal. Without these inputs, we cannot deliver recap and forecasts.

# Where we’re going next
- Taking the design explorations and taking them into flows.
- Finalizing cost of using Ambee for a year-long development and testing of the asthma journal app
- Need to get back to talking to users. Build out a dscout panel and launch. Talk to people around campus — we have two people we talked to that can be active testers as we build
