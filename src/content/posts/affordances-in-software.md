---
title: Affordances in software
pubDate: 2026-08-21
journal: programming-usable-interfaces
---

Physical objects advertise how to use them. A handle looks like something you pull. Software has to fake that.

Buttons look pressable because of contrast, spacing, and a cursor change — not because they are actually raised. When those cues disappear, people hesitate, even if the control still works.

A practical rule: if a control needs a tooltip to be recognized as a control, it is probably under-signaled.
