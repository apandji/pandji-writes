# Interaction Observation

## Tesla Door Unlock

The unlocking of the Tesla door is an intangible interaction that replaces the tangible interaction of using a key fob. People who may have difficulty using it are people without access to a phone. The system senses bluetooth connection and responds by unlocking the door. Errors, and confusions may occur when there is no phone to detect. This interaction centers the phone, and thus this is a limiting factor. 

If I were to redesign this, I would explore ways to sense the specific person. Given that there are cameras on the car, I am curious why it cannot use machine vision to know that it is ME, or other people that we've trained the model on to unlock upon me walking up to the car.
